Our Workflow & Payment Policy

01

Why do we require an initial deposit?

We require a preliminary deposit (25% or 50%) before kicking off any project. This secures your spot in our schedule, covers the initial creative planning, and shows mutual commitment to building something exceptional together.

02

How does the workflow proceed?

Once the initial deposit is confirmed, our team initiates production, design, or development phases. We keep you looped in with progress updates and milestone drafts to ensure everything aligns precisely with your vision.

03

When is the remaining balance paid?

The remaining balance is due only after the project is fully completed, thoroughly reviewed by you, and meets 100% of your satisfaction prior to final delivery of source files and assets.
