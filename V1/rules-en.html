01 / Financial Policy & Retainers Finance
  • Deposit Requirement: A non-refundable deposit ranging between 25% to 50% is mandatory to secure slot booking. No technical work begins without it.
  • Milestone Payments: Remaining balances must be cleared according to pre-agreed milestones before final delivery.
  • Authorized Channels: Payments must be sent strictly via official channels (Vodafone Cash, Instapay, Bank Transfer). The receipt serves as proof.

02 / Workflow & Revisions Limit Production
  • Free Revisions: Every package includes exactly two (2) rounds of structural or technical revisions.
  • Extra Revisions: Any additional changes requested beyond the initial 2 rounds will incur extra charges calculated by hourly rates or fixed fees.
  • Major Rescopes: Fundamental structural changes after production begins require a brand new agreement and schedule.
03 / Raw Assets & Client Responsibility Inputs
  • Asset Quality: Clients hold full responsibility for providing high-resolution, organized raw materials (footage, logos, fonts, audio).
  • Stable Links: Files must be shared via stable cloud links (Google Drive, Mega). Poor source quality is not the studio's liability.
  • Pipeline Pauses: Delayed or missing asset submissions will automatically freeze the project schedule.
04 / Intellectual Property & Files Copyrights
  • Master Files: Rose Studios retains full IP rights to all project source files (.aep, .prproj) unless purchased separately.
  • Source File Purchase: Source files can be acquired for an additional fee equivalent to the base package value after full settlement.
  • Archive Window: Project files are safely archived for up to three (3) months post-delivery before permanent deletion.
05 / Portfolio & Showcase Rights Display
  • Public Display: Rose Studios reserves the right to showcase completed non-disclosure projects and montage cuts in our official portfolio.
  • Attribution: Clients agree to maintain studio attribution when publishing outputs, unless a formal NDA is established beforehand.
06 / Cancellation & Termination Policy
  • Client Cancellation: If a project is cancelled mid-production, the initial deposit is non-refundable to cover reserved resources.
  • Studio Termination: Rose Studios reserves the right to terminate contracts immediately in cases of unprofessional conduct or unresponsive client communication exceeding 14 days.
